## Guardrails

- Always preserve a static baseline.
- Never destructively alter source tracks.
- Never judge creative variants only by loudness.
- Never let novelty override vocal belief.
- Always compare creative variants to the song's emotional truth.

> A mix is finished when the groove is undeniable: the beat carries an identity you can name in one bar, the low end moves as one articulated pocket, and the arrangement leaves space — the silence is as composed as the sound. Sections flip hard enough to feel, and the record breathes between the hits.
